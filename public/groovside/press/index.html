Descriptions

Use whichever length fits. Edit freely for length.

One line

GroovSide is a Mac menu bar app that shows the song playing in Apple Music or Spotify as a record turning on an illustrated turntable, drawn behind the desktop icons.

Short about 50 words

GroovSide shows whatever plays in Apple Music or Spotify as a record turning on a small illustrated turntable behind your Mac's desktop icons. The album cover becomes the label, the tonearm follows the song, and albums are split into LP sides. The longer you listen, the more tiny visitors drop by.

Long about 150 words

GroovSide turns your Mac's desktop into a small listening room. Whatever plays in Apple Music or Spotify appears as a record on an illustrated turntable behind your icons. The album cover becomes the label, and the scene takes on its colors. The tonearm follows the song and leaves a line in the groove. Albums are split into LP sides that are turned over and swapped like real records, and singles play on a 7-inch record. Live radio in Apple Music can play on one record per station. The longer you listen, the more tiny visitors drop by to dance, read and nap around the turntable, while light from a window follows the time of day. Five presets set the look, motion and visitors in one click. There are no accounts, analytics or tracking. GroovSide is made by zinu kim, a solo student developer in Korea. It is a $4.99 one-time purchase on the Mac App Store, coming soon.

Key features

  1. 01
    Every album gets its own recordThe cover becomes the label and the scene takes on its colors. The tonearm follows the song and leaves a line in the groove.
  2. 02
    Real LP sidesUp to six songs a side. When a side ends, the record is turned over or swapped. Singles play on a 7-inch record.
  3. 03
    Rewind like a DJFast-forward or rewind in Apple Music and the record goes with it.
  4. 04
    Knobs that workThe volume knob follows the Mac's volume. Side lamps show which side is playing.
  5. 05
    Little visitorsUp to six drop by to dance, read, doze off and run on the record. When the music stops, they head home.
  6. 06
    Light that follows your daySunlight from a window moves across the desk with the Mac's clock.
  7. 07
    Five presetsClassic, Colorful, Lively, Calm and Minimal set the look, motion and visitors in one click.
  8. 08
    Your colorsColored vinyl in the album's color, or your own colors for the record, background, line, text and tonearm.
  9. 09
    One record per stationLive radio in Apple Music can play on a single record for the whole station.
  10. 10
    Record soundsNeedle drop, motor, a little crackle. Off by default.
  11. 11
    Stays out of the wayMenu bar only, no Dock icon. Icons stay on top and clicks go through. Works on every display, stops drawing when covered, 30 fps on battery.
  12. 12
    Private by designIt only reads what's playing. It never changes your music or opens Music or Spotify by itself.
