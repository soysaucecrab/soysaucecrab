Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

GroovSide is a Mac app that shows the song playing in Apple Music or Spotify as a record on your desktop. It's made by zinu kim, an individual developer in South Korea ("I", "me"), and sold on the Mac App Store.

The short version: the app has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and I don't collect any data about you. What it needs stays on your Mac. It only goes online to look up album track lists and missing album covers.

What the app reads on your Mac

To show the current song, the app asks Music or Spotify (with your permission, through macOS Automation) for the song title, artist, album, album artist, track and disc numbers, length, playback position and album art. It only reads. It doesn't control playback and never opens Music or Spotify.

It also reads your Mac's output volume to turn the volume knob, and your Mac's clock and time zone to set the light from the window.

To save battery, it also checks where other windows sit on your screen (only their position and size, not their titles, contents or which app they belong to) and stops drawing while they cover almost all of the desktop. This isn't stored or sent anywhere. You can turn this off with Pause When Covered in Settings → General.

Your location (optional)

In version 1.0.1 and later, if you turn on Use My Location in Settings → Appearance, the app times the window light and shadows to sunrise and sunset where you are. It stays off unless you turn it on, and macOS asks for your permission first.

  • When you turn it on, and after that about once a day (for example when the app starts or your Mac wakes up), the app asks macOS for your approximate location, rounded to about 10 km before it is stored or used. It doesn't follow where you go.
  • macOS works out the location itself, under Apple's privacy policy. The app only receives the result.
  • The app uses your location only on your Mac, to work out the times of sunrise and sunset. It is never sent to me or anyone else.
  • The app keeps the last location on your Mac, so it can use it the next time it starts without asking again.
  • To stop, turn the setting off, or turn off GroovSide in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Either way the app stops using your location and deletes the saved one, and goes back to using your Mac's time zone.

What the app keeps on your Mac

  • Settings (colors, layout, sounds and so on).
  • Today's songs: the title, artist and time of each song played today, to show the "Played Today" count. It starts over each day.
  • The current record: the lines drawn on the record and which album it belongs to.
  • Album track lists found through the iTunes Search API, up to 500 albums, so they don't have to be looked up again.
  • Your last location, only if you turned on Use My Location: the approximate latitude and longitude macOS last gave the app. It's deleted when you turn the setting off.
  • Purchase check: a one-way fingerprint showing that this Mac has confirmed the purchase (made from an identifier Apple provides for this; the identifier itself isn't kept), and the version and date of your original purchase. The fingerprint lets the app keep opening if a later check fails or you're offline. The original version and date are kept so earlier buyers can be told apart if the pricing ever changes.
  • When to ask for a rating: the date and time you first opened the app, how many days music was playing with it (counted once a day), the last day counted, and the app version it last asked in. It keeps no song details, and Apple decides whether the rating prompt actually appears.

This stays on your Mac and is never sent to me. It's kept in the app's own folder, ~/Library/Containers/kim.zinu.groovside. macOS keeps that folder when you delete the app, so to remove the data too, quit the app and delete that folder.

Buying the app

You buy the app from Apple on the Mac App Store. Apple handles payment, tax, refunds and family sharing under its own terms and the Apple privacy policy. I don't receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple only gives me sales reports that don't identify you.

If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your Mac's settings (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with me. Apple doesn't identify you in these, and I use them only to fix problems and improve the app. You can turn this off at any time.

If you write an App Store review, it is published by Apple under the name you chose, and I can read and reply to it there.

This website

This website is hosted through Cloudflare, which may process your IP address to deliver and protect the site. This site uses no cookies and no analytics.

Email

If you email me, I use your email address and what you write only to reply, and delete the conversation when it's no longer needed. Mail sent to support@zinu.kim is forwarded to my mailbox by Cloudflare, Inc.'s email routing service in the United States.

Where data goes

Apple and Spotify may process the requests above outside South Korea (for example in the United States) under their own policies. I don't transfer any personal information myself, because I don't collect any through the app.

Children

The app isn't directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

The only information I may hold about you is an email conversation, if you sent me one. You can ask me what I have, and ask me to correct or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK, California or South Korea), you may have further rights, including complaining to your local data protection authority. For anything about your purchase, contact Apple. Email me and I'll answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what the app sends, I'll also mention it in the App Store release notes.
