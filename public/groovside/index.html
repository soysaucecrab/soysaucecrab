GroovSide

Now playing, on a turntable.

Whatever you play in Apple Music or Spotify spins as a record on your Mac's desktop, right behind your icons. The cover becomes the label, the needle follows the song, and the longer you listen, the more little visitors drop by.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store $4.99 · macOS 14 or later

Plays like a real record

Every album gets its own record.

The cover becomes the label and the whole scene takes on its colors. The tonearm follows the song and draws a line in the groove as it plays.

Real LP sides

Albums are split into sides of up to six songs. When a side ends, the record is lifted and turned over, or swapped for the next one.

7-inch singles

Singles spin on a smaller record with a bigger label, so a short song doesn't sweep across a whole LP.

Rewind it like a DJ

Fast-forward or rewind in Apple Music and the record speeds up or turns backwards, with an optional spinback sound.

Knobs that mean it

The volume knob follows your Mac's volume, and side lamps show which side is playing.

Company while you listen

The longer you listen, the more friends drop by.

Little visitors show up one by one, up to six at a time. When the music stops, they wave and head home.

  • Dance, headbang and breakdance to the beat
  • Read, doze off, take phone calls, play tag
  • Sometimes they run on the spinning record

Light that follows your day

Morning, noon, sunset, night.

Sunlight through a window moves across the desk with your Mac's clock, and the whole scene shifts its mood.

Make it yours

Colored vinyl, your colors.

Let the album pick the colors, press it on colored vinyl, or choose your own for the record, background, line, text and tonearm. Put the record on the left, center or right.

Stays out of your way

Lives on your desktop, not in your way.

Menu bar only

No Dock icon. Your desktop icons stay on top and clicks go straight through.

Every display

Works on all your screens and steers clear of the menu bar and Dock.

Light on battery

Stops drawing when your screen is locked, asleep or covered, and runs at 30 fps on battery.

Soft record sounds

Needle drop, motor and a gentle crackle in the groove. Off by default.

Gallery

A different scene for every album.

All albums shown are the app's built-in preview albums.

Private by design.

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. GroovSide only reads what's playing. It never changes your music and never opens Music or Spotify by itself. Read the privacy policy.
