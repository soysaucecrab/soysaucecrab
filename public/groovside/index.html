Now Playing

Right on your desktop, behind your icons

Whatever you play spins here, all day. Your icons stay on top, and clicks go straight through to the desktop.

Real LP sides

Up to six songs a side. When a side ends, the record is lifted and turned over, or swapped for the next one.

Every album gets its own record

The cover becomes the label, and the room takes on its colors. The tonearm follows the song and leaves a line in the groove.

7-inch singles

A short song gets a small record with a big label, not a whole LP.

The longer you listen, the more friends drop by

Little visitors show up one by one, up to six at a time. They dance, read, doze off. When the music stops, they wave and head home.

Light that follows your day

Sunlight through a window moves across the desk with your Mac's clock, and the whole scene shifts its mood.

1:00 PM

Colored vinyl, your colors

Let the album pick the colors, press it on colored vinyl, or choose your own for the record, background, line, text and tonearm.

Rewind like a DJ

Fast-forward or rewind in Apple Music and the record goes with it. A spinback sound if you want one.

Snow in December

From December 1 to 26, snow falls behind the turntable, a little tree lights up, and everyone wears a Santa hat. You can turn it off in Settings.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Which music apps does it work with?

    Apple Music (the Music app built into macOS) and Spotify. It reads what’s playing; it never changes your music or opens either app by itself.

    Is it a subscription?

    No. It’s a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store, with no in-app purchases.

    Will it slow my Mac down?

    It stops drawing when the screen is locked, asleep or covered, slows down while the music is paused, and draws at 30 fps on battery.

    What does it know about me?

    Only the song that’s playing, read on your Mac. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

    Are the albums on this page real?

    No. They’re made up, like the preview albums that come with the app, and the music here is made in your browser as you scroll.
