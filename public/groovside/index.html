GroovSide

Now playing, on a turntable.

Whatever you play in Apple Music or Spotify spins as a record on your Mac's desktop, right behind your icons. The cover becomes the label, the needle follows the song, and the longer you listen, the more little visitors drop by.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store $4.99 · macOS 14 or later

Side A

On the record

  1. A1
    Every album gets its own recordThe cover becomes the label, and the room takes on its colors. The tonearm follows the song and leaves a line in the groove.
  2. A2
    Real LP sidesUp to six songs a side. When a side ends, the record is lifted and turned over, or swapped for the next one.
  3. A3
    7-inch singlesA short song gets a small record with a big label, not a whole LP.
  4. A4
    Rewind like a DJFast-forward or rewind in Apple Music and the record goes with it. A spinback sound if you want one.
  5. A5
    Knobs that workThe volume knob follows your Mac’s volume. Side lamps show which side is on.

Who drops by

Little visitors show up one by one, up to six at a time. When the music stops, they wave and head home.

  • Dance, headbang and breakdance to the beat
  • Read, doze off, take phone calls, play tag
  • Sometimes they run on the spinning record

Time of day

Sunlight through a window moves across the desk with your Mac's clock, and the whole scene shifts its mood.

Your colors

Let the album pick the colors, press it on colored vinyl, or choose your own for the record, background, line, text and tonearm. Put the record on the left, center or right.

Side B

Around your desktop

  1. B1
    Menu bar onlyNo Dock icon. Your icons stay on top, and clicks go straight through to the desktop.
  2. B2
    Every displayPlays on all your screens and keeps clear of the menu bar and Dock.
  3. B3
    Easy on batteryStops drawing when the screen is locked, asleep or covered. 30 fps on battery.
  4. B4
    Record soundsNeedle drop, motor hum, a little crackle. Off until you turn it on.
  5. B5
    No accounts, no trackingIt only reads what’s playing. It never changes your music or opens Music or Spotify by itself. Privacy policy.
